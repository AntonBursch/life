R1 — Pure 1D diffusion

Drop a unit pulse in the middle of a closed box. Let it diffuse. Nothing else.

4 0.50

readout

time 0
steps 0
total 0 (should be ≈ 1)
σ (rms spread) 0
σ predicted 0 = √(2 D t)

the claim

A gradient discharges itself by diffusion alone, and the spread grows like √t. Once the gradient is flat, nothing further happens.

what to look for

  • Total mass holds at ~1.0. Zero-flux walls let nothing leave the box. Drift means a numerical bug.
  • σ tracks √(2 D t). The two readouts above should stay close as the field spreads, until the pulse touches the walls and reflects.
  • Quadrupling the time only doubles the spread. Diffusion is a great way to move things short distances and a terrible way to move things long distances. This is why your cells are small.
