the claim

A continuous source at one end and a continuous sink at the other produces a steady non-equilibrium state: a gradient that does not flatten, because flow is being maintained. The field stops changing while energy keeps moving through it.

what to look for

  • The shape settles to a straight line. The analytic steady state is φ(x) = left + (right − left) · x / L. Nothing fancier than that.
  • The field stops moving — but flow doesn’t. Watch the two flux readings converge to the same non-zero number. In = out, but neither is zero.
  • Move a slider and the field re-settles. Steady state is a balance, not a destination.

The value doesn’t matter. Only the difference does. Equal endpoints — at any level — give equilibrium. Nothing flows. Move them apart by any amount and a current appears, with magnitude set entirely by the gap.

This is the same fact that

  • a battery at 5V on both terminals lights no bulb,
  • a river with the same elevation at source and mouth doesn’t run,
  • two reservoirs at the same height connected by a pipe just sit there,
  • the inside of you being warm doesn’t power you — being warmer than the outside does.

Life isn’t powered by energy. The universe is full of energy. Life is powered by differences in energy — by sitting in the pipe between two reservoirs that aren’t at the same height. Earth has the sun on one side (~5800 K) and deep space on the other (~3 K). That gap is what every living thing on this planet is ultimately eating.

why this rung matters

Equilibrium and steady state look alike from outside — both stop moving. They are not the same. An equilibrium costs nothing to maintain. A steady state costs whatever the through-flow is, every second, forever. Every living thing is a steady state. The category is not optional.
