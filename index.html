Substrate-honesty pivot (May 2026). R24–R29 below are marked phenomenological — superseded: they reproduce real behaviour using shortcuts (comparators standing in for bistable chemistry, parameters written from outside, advected labels rather than advected molecules). They stay reachable as the record. Rebuilds (R27′ onward) will replace them with real reaction networks. Standing rule: THESIS.md.

  • R28 — Communication · [phenomenological — superseded; rebuild as R28′] · Phase D, second rung.   • R29 — Convergence · [phenomenological — superseded; rebuild as R29′] · Phase D, third rung. The midline carries a superposition of histories from sources that may no longer exist
  • R27′ — Bistable death · [Phase E · substrate-honest rebuild of R27] · New operator react_field (reaction category — local ODE step, mass-action, non-negative). Replaces R27's latch_field with the Schlögl one-species chemical bistable: dX/dt = k₁A·X² − k₂·X³ − k₃·X + k₄B + D·[u − u_θ]₊. With (6, 1, 11, 6) the bare rate factors as −(X−1)(X−2)(X−3): low stable at X=1, separatrix at X=2, high stable at X=3. The wave activator drives autocatalysis where it peaks; ε = base + gain·(X−1). Same irreversibility R27 showed, earned from real chemistry — kill the wave, X stays in its high attractor on its own, no comparator anywhere
  • R28′ — Bistable communication · [Phase E · substrate-honest rebuild of R28] · No new operator — same composition as R28 (react then advect), applied to an honest substrate. Schlögl species X from R27′ transported by uniform velocity field via the existing advect_by. Wave commits cells on the left; advection carries X mass rightward; downstream cells receive inflow that lifts them past the X=2 separatrix and they commit on their own via local Schlögl dynamics. CLI 128×96 v=2.5 rightward: by t=30, X_high_R≈0.23; by t=400 the spiral has self-extinguished from globally raised ε but X_high_R is still climbing past 0.45, driven entirely by reactant inflow plus local rate law. Communication is mass-action plus transport, no latches, no advected labels
  • R29′ — Bistable convergence · [Phase E · substrate-honest rebuild of R29] · No new operator — same composition as R28′ (react then advect) with position-dependent velocity (left half flows right, right half flows left) and two source reservoirs holding the Schlögl species X clamped at X=3 (honest Dirichlet BC representing continuous metabolic supply). Streams from both sides meet at the midline; CLI 128×96 |v|=2 inward: while clamped, X_hi_mid ≈ 13.5% versus X_hi_L = X_hi_R = 9.1% — the midline commits ~50% higher than either flank because it receives convergent inflow from both directions. Release the clamp: advection drains committed cells faster than the Schlögl rate can hold them; by t=160 the entire grid is back at X=1. R29's "permanent midline record" was a latch_field artifact, not chemistry. Honest open systems require continuous supply — same act, opposite outcome, both substrate-honest
  • R30 — Enclosure · [Phase E · first substrate-honest enclosure] · No new operator — composition of react_field + advect_by + modulate_parameter applied to two coupled species fields (X, B) plus the wave. The Schlögl rate uses a SPATIAL fuel field B in place of the constant reservoir term: where B is high, the bistable’s X=3 fixed point exists; where B is low, only X=1 survives. B is Dirichlet-clamped at the grid edge (open boundary — the “outside”), advected inward by a 4-way radial velocity field, and consumed at first-order rate λ_B everywhere (the downhill that pays for the structure). CLI 128×128 (|v|=1, λ_B=0.1, wall 6 cells, B_supply=1): supply on — wall persists at 18.2% of ring cells, core fraction 0.0%, spiral enclosed inside (excited ≈ 0.21); supply cut at t=160 — by t=180 wall fraction is 0.0%, by t=220 ⟨B⟩ ≈ 0. The boundary is a real chemical structure paid for by a real fuel flow, and it visibly fails when the flow stops. First life/ structure honest enough for ARC rung 5
  • R31 — Autocatalytic wall · [Phase E · the wall builds itself] · No new operator — same alphabet as R30, with modulate_parameter dropped from the loop. No wall seed at t=0; X starts at its low fixed point everywhere. A Barkley spiral kicked once in the centre is the only intervention. As wave fronts sweep through the fuel-rich ring, the drive transiently lifts X past the Schlögl separatrix; in the ring (B large) the high fp X=3 exists, so committed cells STAY committed by mass-action irreversibility; in the fuel-starved core no high fp exists and X relaxes back to 1. The wall is the signature of where B can support it — geometry of commitment = geometry of fuel. CLI 128×128 (|v|=1, λ_B=0.1, D=8, B_supply=1): ring fills from 6.5% (t=4) to 30.0% (t=20) to a steady 33.2% (t=320); core fraction stays at 0.0% past t=10; ⟨B⟩_ring ≈ 0.69 vs ⟨B⟩_core ≈ 0.12.
