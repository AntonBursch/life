the claim

R29 is R28 with a non-uniform velocity field — the same three operators in the same order, on the same substrate. Yet the qualitative behaviour is new: the midline carries a superposition of histories. Cells at the midline have never been visited by either wave, and yet they bear permanent walls built from messages that arrived simultaneously from both directions.
