R4 — Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion

Two chemicals in a square. One is constantly fed in, one is constantly removed, and they react. Under most settings you get soup. Under the right settings the box makes a pattern. This is the first rung where the universe organises itself.

the claim

A box of two reacting chemicals with a steady feed of fuel and a steady removal of product does not relax to a uniform soup. Under the right ratio of feed to kill it organises. Spots replicate, stripes wander, spirals unwind. The universe has just done something it could not do at R1 or R2 or R3: it made a structure with no help.

try this

  1. Start from coral. Hit ↺ Reset then ✦ Seed centre. Watch the V field grow outward in coral-like dendrites until they fill the box. The box never settles. It is permanently busy.
  2. Switch to spots. Reset, seed. After a while you will see V form discrete blobs that pinch and divide. Each blob is a steady "thing" that uses fuel, makes product, and persists.
  3. Switch to maze. Reset, seed. The field forms slow, ropy stripes that thicken and split. Stripe-world is much slower than spot-world.
  4. Click the canvas in spot-world. Each click drops a fresh seed. Watch new spots emerge from the seeds and start dividing on their own.
  5. Slide F or k slowly while a pattern is running. Cross a boundary and watch the pattern break or freeze. Most parameter pairs produce soup. Only a thin slice of the F-k plane produces anything interesting. Life lives in that slice.
  6. Switch to soup. No matter what you do, V decays to zero. The reaction loses to the kill rate. Most boxes in the universe look like this.

what to look for

  • The pattern is not a still photograph. It looks stable but every spot or stripe is being consumed and refreshed every step. Stop the simulation and the picture freezes, but only because we stopped evaluating it; in a real system, stopping the feed dissolves the pattern.
  • The boundary between regimes is sharp. A 0.001 shift in F or k can flip the box from "alive" to "dead." Life is not robust to its own parameters without something else helping it stay in the right window. That something else is what later rungs build.
  • Spots divide. A spot grows, elongates, pinches in the middle, splits. Two spots where one was. This is not biology. It is chemistry plus geometry. Biology will reuse this trick.

why this rung matters

R1 told us closed systems flatten. R2 said a held gradient sustains a current. R3 said the medium can carry stuff faster than diffusion alone. R4 is the first rung where the system uses its flow to make a shape. The shape is not put in by hand. It emerges. It is also entirely conditional on the flow continuing — turn off the feed and the spots vanish.

This is the picture every living thing inherits: a pattern held in shape by a flow that does not stop. The pattern isn't substance. It is a process. Cut the supply and there is no smaller pattern, no still pattern; there is just absence.
