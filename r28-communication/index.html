R28 — Communication

Phase D, second rung. No new operator. R28 is built entirely out of pieces we already have: latch_field composed with advect_by composed with latch_field. Each cell maintains a local latch (R27's wall_state) and a transmitted latch whose state is the advected, accumulated shadow of every local latch upstream. The wave's ε is modulated by the transmitted field, not the local one. So each cell experiences walls that originated some time ago at position x − v·t. Walls travel without their builder. That is the minimum signature of a communication channel.

the claim

Information can travel without mass. R6 (advect_by) moves a scalar through space; R27 (latch_field) makes a state irreversibly committed. Compose them in this order — latch, then advect, then latch again — and the second latch becomes a permanent record of upstream commitments. The medium has not moved; the wave has not crossed; yet the right half of the grid is full of walls whose only origin is a spiral on the left. Same alphabet, no new operator, qualitatively new behaviour: a channel.

CLI verified, 128×96, v=2.5 cells/t.u., dt=0.02. At tissue time 30, the right half shows wall_local = 7.1% but transmitted = 100%. The 93% gap is everything the channel has delivered that the medium itself has not yet built. Kill the wave at t=60 and the channel keeps delivering: transmitted stays at 100% in the right half indefinitely. The spiral is dead; its words are still arriving.

try this

  1. Default. A spiral on the left builds wall_local in its footprint. Watch panel 4: a slow curtain of transmitted walls drifts rightward at velocity 0.5 cells/t.u. The wave on the left starts seeing its own walls coming back at it from the right.
  2. Kill wave. wall_local stops growing. transmitted keeps marching. The dead spiral's prior commitments continue to arrive downstream.
  3. Spark right. After letting the channel run, kill the wave and spark a fresh spiral on the right. It is born inside walls it did not build — walls that arrived as messages from a spiral that no longer exists.
  4. Velocity = 0. Disables the channel. The transmitted field is now just a second copy of wall_local — no transport, no communication. Confirm: the right half stays empty.
  5. Velocity = 5.0. The channel floods the whole grid in seconds. The wave gets ε-killed everywhere within ~30 t.u. — the message arrives faster than the system can contain it. Communication has bandwidth, and turning it up has consequences.
  6. Clear walls. Wipes memory, wall_local, and transmitted. The only way to silence the channel is from outside the substrate.
